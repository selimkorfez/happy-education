## The rules we work to

We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), which is the law that governs cookies and marketing messages.

Many of the people we work with live in Türkiye. Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) can also apply to them. Section 12 sets out what that means and where the two regimes differ.

## The personal data we hold

**Data you give us when you get in touch.** Your name, email address, phone number, the country you are in, the language you prefer, and whatever you tell us about what you are looking for.

**Consultation and appointment data.** The date and time of your appointment, how it was held, notes of what was discussed, and the actions we agreed.

**Application support data.** Where you ask us to help with an application, this can include your date of birth, nationality, passport details, address history, academic transcripts and certificates, English or other language test results, references, personal statements, financial evidence, and details of family members named on an application.

**Health, disability and other special category data.** Some applications ask about a medical condition, a disability, a dietary requirement or a learning need. This is "special category" data and we only handle it where you have given us explicit consent to pass it on, or where the law otherwise allows it.

**Payment data.** The amount, date, service, invoice details and the outcome of the payment. Card payments are processed by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number, expiry date or security code.

**Newsletter and marketing data.** Your email address, the language you chose, whether you opened or clicked a message, and the record of when and how you subscribed or unsubscribed.

**Website and technical data.** Your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you viewed, the site that referred you, and your cookie choices. Where analytics cookies are allowed, we also receive aggregated usage statistics.

**Security and log data.** Records of requests to our website and to our forms, kept so we can detect and block abuse.

**Records of your contact with us.** Emails, WhatsApp and social media messages, call notes, and complaint files.

## Where the data comes from

Most of it comes from you. Some comes from a parent or legal guardian applying on behalf of someone under 18. Some comes back to us from a school, college or university you have asked us to contact, for example an offer letter or a request for a missing document. Technical data is generated automatically when you use the website.

**Anyone we are legally required to tell.** A court, a regulator, or the police where the law obliges us to disclose.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Sending data outside the UK

Some of our suppliers are based outside the UK, or store data outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on one of these safeguards:

- the country is covered by UK adequacy regulations, which means the government has decided it offers equivalent protection
- the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses
- your explicit consent, or the transfer being necessary to perform your contract, where no other route is available

Türkiye is not currently covered by UK adequacy regulations. If we need to send your information to a colleague or contact in Türkiye, we rely on the transfer being necessary to perform the contract you asked us for, or on your explicit consent. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguard that applies to any particular transfer.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of the personal data we hold about you
- **correct** anything that is wrong or incomplete
- **delete** it, where we no longer have a reason to keep it
- **restrict** what we do with it while a question about it is resolved
- **transfer** it to you or to someone else in a machine readable format, where we hold it on the basis of consent or a contract
- **stop** processing it where we rely on legitimate interests and you object

You can **withdraw consent** at any time where consent is our basis, for example for marketing or analytics. That does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

You can **object to direct marketing** at any time and we must stop. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also just email us.

To use any of these rights, email [admin@happyeducation.uk](mailto:admin@happyeducation.uk). We will reply within one month. If the request is complicated we can extend that by up to two further months, and we will tell you why within the first month. There is no charge. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your information to somebody else.

## If you are in Türkiye: KVKK

Turkish law calls the data controller the **veri sorumlusu**. For the services described here, the veri sorumlusu is HAPPY EDUCATION CONSULTANCY LTD, at the address in section 1.

Turkish law (Law No. 6698, KVKK) works differently from UK GDPR in ways that matter to you:

- **Consent has a bigger role.** KVKK starts from explicit consent (açık rıza) and then lists the situations where consent is not needed, such as processing that is directly necessary for a contract you are party to. UK GDPR treats consent as one basis among six.
- **Special categories are handled separately.** Health data has its own rules under Article 6 of KVKK.
- **Transfers abroad have their own regime.** Article 9 of KVKK, as amended in 2024, allows transfer on the basis of an adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards such as a standard contract notified to the Kurum, or one of the listed exceptions including explicit consent.
- **The complaint route is different.** Under KVKK Article 13 you apply to us first. We must answer within 30 days. If we refuse, or do not answer in time, you can complain to the Kurul within 30 days of learning our answer and in any event within 60 days of applying.
- **Your rights under Article 11** cover learning whether your data is processed, asking what it is used for, asking for correction or deletion, asking us to notify third parties of a correction, objecting to a result produced by automated analysis, and claiming compensation for damage.

Whether a UK company that serves customers in Türkiye must register with **VERBİS** and appoint a Turkish representative depends on facts we are confirming with a Turkish data protection lawyer. We will state the position here once it is settled. We are not claiming a registration we do not hold.

## Keeping your data safe

We use encrypted connections, restrict access to client files to the people who need them, and protect our forms against automated abuse. No system is perfectly secure, so please help us: never send us a password, and never send sensitive documents through a public form or a social media message.

We will never ask you to send money to a personal bank account, and we will never email you asking you to change payment details. If you receive a message like that claiming to be from us, do not act on it. Call us on the number published on this website.
