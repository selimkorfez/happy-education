## Prices

Every price is shown to you in writing before you pay, together with what it covers. We do not add anything you have not seen.

Prices are quoted in pounds sterling unless we say otherwise. Where we show a price in another currency for convenience, the payment is taken in the currency stated at the checkout.

Where VAT applies to your service, the amount is shown before you pay and appears separately on your invoice.

If a price on this website is obviously wrong, we will contact you before taking payment or, if payment has been taken, we will tell you and you can either confirm at the correct price or cancel for a full refund.

## What our fee covers, and what it does not

Our fee covers the work described in your written summary.

It does not cover money owed to somebody else. Those costs are separate, are set by them, and change without notice. They typically include:

- institution application fees, deposits and tuition
- accommodation deposits and rent
- visa application fees, biometric appointment fees and the immigration health surcharge
- language tests and other examinations
- document translation, certification and legalisation
- courier and postage
- travel and insurance

We will tell you which of these apply to your plan as early as we can, and we will tell you the published amount and where it comes from. We do not add a mark up to a third party cost. If we ever charge a handling fee for arranging one, it is shown separately.

## How to pay

Card payments are handled by **Stripe**, our payment processor. Your card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number, expiry date or security code. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment through your banking app, which is a security requirement, not an extra step we have added.

We will tell you when payment is due. Where a service runs over a period, we may agree instalments and set out the dates in writing.

If your bank charges you a fee for an international payment, or applies its own exchange rate, that is between you and your bank. Please send the full amount so the invoice clears.

## Failed and late payments

If a payment fails, we will tell you and ask you to try again. We do not charge a penalty for a failed payment.

If a fee remains unpaid after we have reminded you, we may pause work on your case and tell you what is outstanding. We will not withhold documents that belong to you. If we take longer to restart than the pause lasted, we will explain why.

We may charge statutory interest on a genuinely overdue business debt, but we do not apply late payment charges to consumers beyond what the law allows.

## Paying institutions

Wherever the institution allows it, **pay them directly**. It is safer for you, the payment is traceable to your name, and their receipt is your proof.

If an institution's process requires the payment to come through us, we will say so in writing, tell you exactly what we will pass on and when, and send you the institution's receipt when we have it. We do not hold your money any longer than the transfer takes, and we are not a regulated holder of client money, so a direct payment is always the better option where one exists.

## Receipts and invoices

You get a receipt for every payment to us, and an invoice on request. Tell us if you need the invoice made out to a parent, a sponsor or an employer, and we will do it before payment rather than reissuing afterwards.

## Protecting yourself from payment fraud

This matters, so please read it.

- **We will never ask you to send money to a personal bank account.**
- **We will never email or message you to say our bank details have changed.** If you receive a message like that, it is not from us, however convincing it looks.
- Before making any transfer, call us on the number published on this website, which you have typed in yourself, and confirm the details out loud. Do not use a number from the email you are checking.
- We will never ask for your card's security code, your online banking password or a one time passcode. Nobody legitimate will.

If you think you have been targeted, contact your bank immediately and then tell us, so we can warn others.

## Refunds

Our refund and cancellation policy explains your 14 day right to cancel, what happens if you asked us to start straight away, how refunds are calculated after that period, and which third party costs cannot be returned once they have been paid out.

Refunds are made to the card or account the payment came from. We do not charge a fee for making a refund.

## Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please tell us first. A chargeback raised without contacting us usually takes longer to resolve than a conversation and can freeze work on your application. Raising a chargeback does not affect any statutory right you have, and we will not treat it as a reason to end our agreement if the underlying complaint is genuine.
