## Selection rules

- Approved CMS photos remain the first choice in page heroes. Both CMS and catalogue approval must be the boolean `true`. A withheld CMS image cannot supply the fallback’s alt text.
- The homepage has its own Cambridge riverside photograph and correctly labels the location. The supporting copy sits below the photo, leaving its subject and credit unobscured.
- Section indexes have individually chosen photos. Guide/service detail heroes now use their title, including pages without an uploaded image.
- The launch English article/guide/service collection has explicit editorial selections, so adjacent cards differ. The title controls selection consistently across cards, related links and details.
- Other content checks the title before secondary category/excerpt text. City references remain geographic; practical topics (applications, budgets, languages, accommodation, family guidance and travel) use suitable editorial pools. English and Turkish place/topic aliases are recognised.
- General country stories draw from several relevant locations within that country. Country landing pages retain their documentary destination image; a specific city wins over a broader country label.
- Multiple-place comparisons and unknown subjects use neutral study photography. The system does not randomly assign a foreign campus to an unsupported location. Exact institution photography remains controlled by the existing institution registry.
- Selection is deterministic, with no request-global rotation or random client state. Similar topics can appropriately share a photo; the system does not claim every content record has a unique commissioned image.

The 46 existing Wikimedia source files (including the England image) were also rechecked against Commons on 9 September 2026: all resolved and their reported reuse licences matched the supported CC/CC0 registry types. Newly bundled photographs do not depend on remote image redirects at page-load time.
