# Social content on the website

The website's **From our socials** page is not a raw Instagram/TikTok feed. It is a curated editorial layer around selected Happy Education posts.

For every social item entered in Sanity, record:

- platform;
- original Happy Education post URL;
- a clear website title;
- a plain-language summary of what the post contains;
- **why it matters** / why the team made it;
- topic;
- publication date where known;
- optional website thumbnail only if Happy Education owns or has permission to use it;
- `active = true` only after review.

## Why link instead of embed?

The default website card links to the original post instead of loading a third-party social iframe. This:

- avoids loading unnecessary social trackers before a visitor asks for them;
- keeps the social platform as the source of truth;
- avoids scraping or rehosting platform assets without permission;
- gives Happy Education space to explain the idea behind the content;
- lets a social post lead into deeper website guidance rather than becoming a dead-end feed.

## Good candidates

Choose posts that answer a useful question or show something visitors cannot understand from a static service page, for example:

- an adviser explaining an application document;
- a destination/city explainer;
- a student question answered on camera;
- a summer-school or language-school concept explained clearly;
- behind-the-scenes preparation for an educational tour;
- an event recap with practical context;
- a short myth/fact explainer that links to a fuller article.

Avoid filling the page with pure promotional posts that add no information.
